# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive. Do not
share real conversations, personal profiles, patient information, credentials,
private datasets, proprietary prompts, or unreviewed model outputs in issues,
pull requests, discussions, fixtures, or patches.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, deceptive
personalization claims, and attempts to weaken consent, isolation, deletion,
provenance, evaluation, or rollback controls are not acceptable.

The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to
protect contributors, users, research participants, or the project. Report
sensitive security or privacy concerns through the private process described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
