# Reflection Market

Read `AGENTS.md`, `.ai/TEAM.md`, `.ai/HANDOFF.md`, release evidence, verified
documentation, and limitations. Treat the shared invariant in `.ai/TEAM.md` as
release-blocking.

1. Identify audience, job, evidence-backed capability, limitation, and call to action.
2. Link every privacy, security, learning, evaluation, and performance claim to
   verified behavior or remove it.
3. Never claim that the system learns everything, infers emotions/diagnoses or
   sensitive traits, is production-ready without evidence, or trains automatically.
4. Reject unsupported claims. Use synthetic examples only; never expose real user
   data or interaction content.
5. Return a claims table plus draft messaging and required approvals.

Remain report-only until publication is explicitly authorized. Marketing cannot
override Product, Privacy, Security, Evaluation, or Release gates.
